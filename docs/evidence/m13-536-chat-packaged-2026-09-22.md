## What was driven (flow evidence — `bun run test:e2e:chat`)

1. **Canonical conversation surface.** Header names the runtime session and
   generation, status "Live"; transcript renders 5 tiered rows (1 user, 2
   tool, 2 assistant) from bounded payload fields; composer enabled with the
   generation-send status, Send clears the draft, busy surface offers Stop
   and Steer.
2. **Streaming row stability.** The fixture appends canonical events when the
   spec dispatches its window event (no wall-clock race): 5 → 6 → 7 rows, the
   pre-append first row remains the same mounted DOM node (the "preserve live
   transcript rows during streaming" slice), and repeated delivery keeps
   appending without disturbing earlier rows.
3. **Attention gating.** With a pending approval the composer is disabled and
   states the exact reason ("Waiting for approval before sending input.");
   Approve/Deny render enabled; the question row requires a non-empty answer
   before Submit answer enables; resolution does not throw or unmount the
   pending rows.
4. **Sequence-gap recovery.** A `resync_required` transcript renders the
   "Transcript gap detected" alert, the Reconnect transcript button, the
   Disconnected status, and the composer's unavailable reason; reconnecting
   re-attaches and the gap is still reported truthfully.
5. **Dev↔Chat switch preservation (window-level proof).** Mirrors the
   model-layer proof (`chat-conversation-model.test.ts`, "Dev↔Chat repeated
   switching preserves the canonical session, draft, sequence, and
   scrollback") in the real window: three full cycles across the rail switch
   with the chat surface mounted between Dev visits. Every store-backed
   session fact survives each cycle — the selected session (`aria-current`,
   plus its `devProject`/`devSession` URL mirror), the collapsed Product
   group, and the unknown-param URL contract (`sentinel=keep`,
   `devE2e=preserved`). Nothing relaunches or resets.

Result: **5/5 passed**, three consecutive clean runs (9.6–13.5 s per run),

## Defect found and fixed (fix + test together)

`packages/dev-view/src/chat/chat.css` laid the chat grid out as
`auto minmax(0, 1fr) auto` for exactly three children (header, transcript,
composer). When a reconnect/resync notice renders between header and
transcript, the notice stole the `minmax(0, 1fr)` track, collapsed to zero
height, and the transcript section painted over the notice — **the "Reconnect
transcript" button was pointer-blocked** (Playwright: "`<p>Transcript gap
detected…</p>` … intercepts pointer events", retried 170×). A real owner could
not click the recovery affordance whenever the surface showed it.

Fix: the transcript and composer now own their tracks by explicit grid-line
placement (`grid-template-rows: auto auto minmax(0, 1fr) auto` plus
`grid-row` pins on `> .dev-chat__stream` and `> .dev-chat__composer`); the
reserved second track collapses to zero height when no notice renders, so the
no-notice layout is pixel-identical to before. Regression coverage: the
reconnect flow journey clicks the previously blocked button (test 4), and the
reconnect visual baselines pin the corrected composition.
