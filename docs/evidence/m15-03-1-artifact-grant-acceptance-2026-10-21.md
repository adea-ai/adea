durable grant store landed through the merged PR #1207, and the publication/retrieval gates

## Acceptance clause 1 — check exact artifact version/audience at publication and retrieval; a private URL cannot grant access

| Requirement                                                                           | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Coverage                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| a locator alone is never an access grant — a private URL grants nothing               | the same policy: a locator-only target resolves to `null` evidence and is refused at both gates                                                                                                                      | "a locator without current evidence proves nothing: no URL or artifact id grants access", "a grant that binds a different target is a forged locator", "a known grant identity relabelled onto another artifact is refused"                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| the audience is checked against the registration, never assumed                       | the registered audience workspace, compared at decision time                                                                                                                                                         | "only the registered audience workspace may retrieve", "a self-referencing audience is not a cross-workspace reference", "audience removal refuses while the grant revision itself is current"                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
